# Welcome to My Digital Education Portfolio

**Secondary Design & Technology | Creativity and Independent Learning**

I studied Interaction Design at the University of Sydney, where I gained a deep understanding of user experience and design. Currently, I am a pre-service teacher in the Design and Technology program, and I take great joy in seeing students express their unique ideas and creativity through their own work.

In one of my classes, the assignment was for students to design their own patterns on pre-made wooden boxes. I encouraged them to create original designs and avoid using images found online. This was done both to foster students' awareness of copyright and to spark their creativity. One student designed a pizza-shaped piece, illustrating different foods on each slice. I believe this is a great example of how students can transform a simple idea into a truly remarkable piece of work.

I believe artificial intelligence can help students turn their ideas into reality. Some students, though creative, struggle to express themselves through drawing. AI can help them visualize these ideas. However, students should first provide the AI with their own ideas and specific details, rather than simply asking it to generate a series of images and then selecting from them. In this way, students can truly demonstrate their own thinking and creative process.
